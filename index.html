How It Works
01

Pick a guide

Choose any book in the archive. Start with TROJAN or wait for the one that calls to you. Each guide is self-contained and requires no prior knowledge.

02

One day at a time

Read one entry per morning. Each day delivers one core idea, one real-world example showing it in action, and one exercise to apply before tomorrow.

03

Do the exercise

This is the non-negotiable part. The exercise is not optional. It is the mechanism. Ideas without application are just more conditioning.

04

Return on Day 30

Go back to Day 1. Re-read your first answers. Notice the gap between what you saw then and what you see now. That gap is the only measure of progress that matters.

About BookDecoded and how the guides work

What is BookDecoded?+
BookDecoded publishes structured 30-day study guides for the most influential books on power, influence, psychology, and self-command. Each guide breaks a book into one theme per day — with a real-world example and a single actionable exercise. The guides are free to read online. Companion workbooks (print-ready PDFs) are available for $8.
Do I need to own the book to use the guide?+
No. Each guide is self-contained. You can work through it without owning the book — the guide extracts and applies the core ideas independently. That said, reading the book alongside the guide produces significantly deeper results, and each guide links directly to the book on Amazon.
How are the guides different from book summaries?+
Book summaries tell you what a book says. BookDecoded guides make you do something with it. Every day ends with a real exercise — a question to answer, a behavior to practice, a pattern to observe in your own life. The structure is designed to move ideas from the page into lived experience over 30 days.
Are the guides really free?+
Yes. The online guides are completely free. You enter your email at the halfway point to unlock the second half of each guide — this lets us notify you when new guides launch. Companion workbooks (formatted PDF versions with journal space for each exercise) are available for $8 each on Payhip.
When will the other guides launch?+
New guides are published regularly. The 48 Laws of Power, Influence by Cialdini, and Atomic Habits are currently in production. Enter your email in the notification form above to be alerted the moment each one goes live — subscribers get 48 hours of early access before public launch.
Can I suggest a book?+
Yes. The archive is built around books that reward this kind of slow, applied study — typically in the areas of power, influence, psychology, wealth, strategy, and philosophy. If there's a book you'd like to see decoded, email hello@bookdecoded.com with the title and why you think it belongs here.
